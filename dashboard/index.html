Seven filters, read from 4Founders’ published investment criteria

C1Ticket fitMarket cap EUR 5–80M, so a EUR 100K–4M ticket is 5–20% of the company. Criterion 03
C2Strong tech componentTechnology sector, or the agent shows, with its source, that technology is the product. Criterion 02
C3ScaleRevenue CAGR over 3 years ≥ 20%. Criterion 02
C4OwnershipFounders and management hold ≥ 20% of the shares. Criterion 01
C5International mindset≥ 25% of revenue outside Spain, read from the segment note. Criterion 01
C6AmbitionA published growth plan with quantified, dated targets. Criterion 01
C7Clean accountsAudit opinion clean, no going-concern doubt. Hygiene.

The rule the agents cannot break. No agent can give a PASS on a figure the verifier has not found on its page. A missing figure is NEEDS DATA, never a pass. The verifier is plain code, so the check does not depend on which AI you run it with.
