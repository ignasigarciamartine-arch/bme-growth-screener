Method

Criteria first

The eight filters and their thresholds were fixed on 25 Jul 2026, before the universe was examined, in config/criteria.toml. Thresholds are never edited in place; a change is appended to the changelog with its date and reason and applies to every company, not only to survivors.

  • 2026-07-28, F6: Definition made precise; threshold unchanged at 15%. Free float excludes (a) founders and controlling holders, (b) directors and senior management, (c) employees and partner vehicles, (d) treasury stock. Financial investors without a board seat or employment link count as free float, even above 5%. The undefined term decided whole cases (one company read 14.5% or 22.0% depending on it). Applied retroactively to every screened company, not only to survivors.

Missing data

A filter that cannot be evaluated is NEEDS_DATA, never PASS, and one NEEDS_DATA keeps a company out of the passes. A bound on free float built from holdings the definition excludes can fail F6 but never pass it.

Where figures come from

auto: extracted by the code on this run (market cap from the BME API, Lighthouse tables, the opinion regex), 45 filter inputs. documented: read page by page and versioned in data/extracted/ with file, page and method, 94 inputs. Accounts come before Lighthouse sheets, and a sheet is used for P&L figures only for the same fiscal year. Each documented reading is looked for again on its page on every run (text layer, then local OCR).

Reproducible

This page is written by the pipeline itself: python -m screener run --as-of 2026-10-28 reads the versioned snapshot data/snapshots/2026-10-28/, downloads the public documents it lists and gives the same verdicts on any machine. Code: commit 601ba88.

Screening of public filings only: annual accounts and significant-holdings filings from the BME Growth API, and Lighthouse sheets (institutodeanalistas.com). No contact with any company, no paid databases. Not investment advice and not a valuation.
